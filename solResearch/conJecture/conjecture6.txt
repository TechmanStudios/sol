SOL Resonant-Gated Multi-Substrate Manifold Memory Conjecture (Conjecture 6)
============================================================================

Conjecture:
A hierarchical multi-substrate manifold (FMSM) system containing a parent coordinator 
and multiple independent child specialist pockets can function as a multi-bit, 
frequency-addressable analog register bank by synthesizing the physical properties 
of waveguide resonance, dynamic wormhole shuttering, active battery latching, 
and transient DRAM-like pressure discharge recall.

Specifically:
1. **Frequency-Selective Write Routing**: 
   Driving the parent coordinator with soliton waves tuned to the exact bandpass 
   resonance of target child waveguides (e.g., Freq A = 3.2725, Freq B = 6.0000) 
   selectively delivers wave energy and belief to the target child pockets.
2. **Independent Active Battery Latches**: 
   Each specialist pocket maintains an independent active memristive battery latch 
   (e.g., childA_node_0000, childB_node_0000). Upon receiving threshold belief, 
   the target battery flips to state 1.0, locking the analog state in that specific pocket.
3. **Decoupled Cross-Talk-Free Hold**: 
   Severing all parent-child wormhole conduits during the hold phase traps wave energy 
   in the pockets with zero cross-talk, allowing the registers to maintain their 
   respective states independently.
4. **Sequential DRAM-like Recall**: 
   Dynamically and sequentially reopening the parent-child wormholes discharges 
   trapped register energy back to the parent coordinator, producing distinct, 
   addressable transient readout pulses. Active latches prevent vacuum advection collapse, 
   ensuring smooth readouts.
